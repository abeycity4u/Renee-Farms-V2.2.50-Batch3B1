--- RELEASE_V2.2.47_MORTALITY_VALUATION_REFINEMENT.txt ---
Renee Farms V2.2.47 — Mortality Valuation Refinement

Decision:
- Layer/Broiler mortality remains recorded and valued using the explicit cycle Bird Cost Basis.
- Mortality Value is management intelligence for productive biological assets lost.
- Mortality Value is NOT added to Feed Consumed, Other Operating Cost, Total Operating Cost, or period Profit/Loss.
- No expense transaction is created by mortality.
- Profit/Loss remains Revenue - Feed Consumed - Other Operating Cost.
- Per-bird, per-egg, and per-crate operating economics use operating cost only.
- Profit/Bird and Profit Margin use the same period Profit/Loss.
- Missing Bird Cost Basis leaves mortality valuation incomplete without inventing a value and without changing Profit/Loss.

Rationale:
Layer birds are productive biological assets held to produce eggs over their productive cycle. Mortality reduces productive capacity and is tracked as a flock/asset KPI. Charging the original cost basis again as a period expense can double-count acquisition cost and does not represent a new cash/operating transaction.
